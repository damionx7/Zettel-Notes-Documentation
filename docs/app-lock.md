title: App Lock
tags:
  - security
---

To lock the app, go to `Settings > Security`, select **App Lock** and choose **Passcode**. Enter a passcode and confirm. You will be asked for it whenever the app restarts or the lock timeout expires.

!!! info

    To unlock with your fingerprint, enable **Biometrics** on the App Lock screen.


To change the timeout, adjust **Lock Timeout (in minutes)**. The maximum is 30 minutes.

When a timeout is set, a lock button appears on the notes screen. Tap it to lock the app immediately.
